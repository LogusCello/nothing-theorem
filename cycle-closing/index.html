The reversible state and the lossy reading

A sharper boundary

All nonempty strings of 1s are fixed. Any string made only of 1 and 5 reaches one of those fixed strings in at most one step.

Any string containing another digit grows without a finite length bound. The subsystem {2, 3, 4, 7, 9, 6, 8} never disappears; every lineage in it reaches a branching digit within four substitutions. Digit 0 enters it through 0 → 2.

5 → 11 → 11
15 → 111 → 111
2 → 3 → 4 → 7 → 29

Your “frozen / growing” distinction survives. Its exact boundary is {1, 5} versus the remaining digits, rather than “1 versus everything else.”
